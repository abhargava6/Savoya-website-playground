This email is a follow-up with the impact analysis — comparing last night's new-logic scores against the previous night's old-logic scores across all 765 active vendors.

Key Takeaway

The shift at go-live in overall scores is small because it's corrective in nature — recalculating the last 90 days of historical data under improved logic. The average overall score shifted just -0.09 points. But this understates the real impact for two reasons:

1. Scorecards show a bigger story. The individual metrics vendors see and act on — closeout rates, app usage, onsite marking — are dropping significantly. Average vendor portal usage dropped from 74% to 71%, and closeout rates fell from 100% to 86%. These are the numbers that drive vendor behavior.

2. Future trips will compound the effect. As vendors complete new trips under the new logic, each trip's performance will directly influence their score. A single fault case can drop a vendor's score by 0.5 to 7 points depending on their trip volume. Over the coming weeks, vendors who perform well will separate sharply from those who don't.

Overall Score Distribution: Before & After

The overall distribution shape is nearly identical — the new logic recalibrates individual vendors without shifting the entire curve. Mean moved from 14.09 to 14.00; median from 16.61 to 16.52.

 

Factor-Level Impact

Of the 13 recalculated factors, ASAP Trips is the largest positive mover (+2.47 avg, 68% affected), followed by Vehicle Age Adjustment (+1.35 avg, 38% affected) — rewarding vendors operating sprinters, coach buses, and newer vehicles. Vendor Closeouts (-1.80, 52% affected) and Marked Onsite (-1.71, 49% affected) are the biggest drops, reflecting stricter validation of actual app actions. Forward-Looking: Why Scores Will Shift More Over Time

Today's small overall shift reflects a one-time correction of 90 days of historical data. Going forward, every new trip a vendor completes will be scored under the new logic from the start. As vendors perform trips — good or bad — the new scoring will compound and overall scores are expected to diverge significantly between high and low performers.

What one bad trip costs a vendor

Under new logic, a single fault case on a future trip can drop a vendor's overall score by 0.5 to 7 points — the impact scales inversely with trip volume. Smaller vendors feel it more.

What good performance earns

Conversely, vendors who consistently use the app fully, close out on time, arrive early, and accept ASAP trips will see their scores climb. Each point of app usage credit contributes roughly +0.6 points to overall score. Each point of on-time onsite credit adds +0.3 points. Vendors doing the right things on every trip will steadily pull ahead.

⭐  Bottom line: The go-live correction is the floor, not the ceiling. As the 90-day rolling window fills with trips scored under new logic, we expect to see meaningful score separation between vendors. The scoring system now has the precision to reward great performance and penalize poor performance on a per-trip basis — something the old system couldn't do.

 

Perceived Benefits vs. Actual Results

We scoped this project around four expected business outcomes. Here's how the first-run data validates each one.

✓

Confirmed

Reducing manual score corrections

56 vendors (7.3%) saw their scores move more than 2 points — these are cases where old logic was producing inaccurate scores that likely required manual override. 9 vendors previously scoring below 5 were corrected upward; 9 scoring above 15 were corrected downward. The ops team should see a meaningful reduction in manual score adjustment requests.
✓

Confirmed

Penalizing vendors for fault cases

117 vendors with fault cases averaged a -0.56 point delta vs. -0.01 for the 648 clean vendors — a 50x difference. 24 vendors saw more fault cases attributed under the new logic as case detection became more precise. This creates a real financial incentive for vendors to reduce case-generating behavior.
✓

Confirmed

Accurate app usage credit

47% of vendors (359) saw their mobile app usage factor change. 212 vendors lost credit vs. 147 who gained — the new logic is deliberately stricter. If the driver didn't take all the required actions in the app, the vendor no longer gets partial credit. Average app factor dropped 1.71 points. Similarly, 49% of vendors saw their onsite marking factor recalculated, with 231 losing credit vs. 147 gaining.
✓

Confirmed

Rewarding ASAP acceptance & hard-to-fill work

This is the single strongest signal in the entire overhaul. 517 vendors (68%) gained ASAP credit — zero vendors lost credit. Average ASAP factor increased +3.66 points, the largest positive shift of any factor. Even with the weight reduced from 3 to 1 to prevent outsized impact, this factor is effectively rewarding vendors who step up for short-notice trips. Additionally, 280 vendors saw improved on-time onsites credit (vs. only 31 who decreased).
✓

Confirmed

Rewarding specialty vehicles & vehicle quality

The Vehicle Age Adjustment factor is the second-largest positive mover in the entire overhaul at +1.35 avg points. 256 vendors (34%) gained credit for operating sprinters, coach buses, and newer vehicles — only 31 (4%) saw a decrease. The top beneficiaries are high-volume vendors running specialty fleets, with gains up to +72 points. This factor creates a direct incentive for vendors to invest in vehicle quality and maintain specialty inventory.
