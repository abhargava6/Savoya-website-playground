Vendor Scoring Phase 1
We've overhauled 12 of 18 vendor performance scoring factors to fix data quality issues, eliminate unfair penalties, and introduce graduated scoring. The feature flag has been turned on — new scores begin computing at 1:00 AM tonight.

Expected Business Impact
● Cost savings from eliminating manual score corrections
No more manually adjusting scores or spending time explaining inaccurate numbers to vendors. The scores are now trustworthy out of the box.
● Reduced case rate through real accountability
Vendors are now properly penalized for cases — driving behavior change, reducing trip volume from problem vendors, and lowering the overall case rate.
● Right credit for app usage — no more free passes
Vendors who didn't complete all required actions in the app no longer get credit. Event-based validation ensures the app was actually used through the proper workflow.
● Rewarding vendors for hard-to-fill work
Enhanced ASAP, Vehicle Age, and other factors now properly reward vendors for accepting short-notice trips and providing specialty vehicles like Sprinters, Executive Vans, and Coach Buses.
01
Data Quality & Accuracy
Fixed counting errors, wrong database fields, and actions wrongly attributed to vendors instead of internal staff.
● Vendor Fault Cases — now counts all fault cases regardless of assignment status changes
● Cases w/ Compensation — fixed data field: now correctly reads client compensation, not vendor compensation
● Marked Onsite — fixed double-counting bug at the reservation level
● Mobile App Used — upgraded from flag check to event-based validation
02
Fairness to Vendors
Vendors are no longer penalized for canceled trips, no-shows, and other situations outside their control. Internal actions no longer inflate vendor scores.
● On-Time Onsites — canceled trips now receive full credit
● Driver Had Photo — canceled trips where driver never marked onsite get full credit
● Vendor Closeouts — now only counts vendor/driver/TC roles; internal team actions excluded
● On-Time Closeouts — same role restriction applied
03
Granularity & Coverage
Graduated scoring replaces binary pass/fail, and specialty vehicles that were previously excluded are now included with their own scoring brackets.
● ASAP Trips — changed from binary to graduated scoring (0.25–1.0 points within a 24-hour window)
● Vehicle Age — now covers Sprinter, Executive Van, Mini Bus, and Coach Bus with own age brackets
● Late & Very Late Onsites — no logic change; regression coverage confirmed
