Referral Program

Share Savoya

Invite your friends and colleagues with just a few clicks. Gift your network $50 off of their first trip. Earn up to $500 for each referral that completes three trips within six months.

Get Started

How It Works

Step 1

Invite

Invite friends and colleagues to Savoya using your unique share link. Share with a few clicks by copying and pasting your code, or use one of our prepopulated email, social media, or text messages.

Step 2

Give

Gift your connections peace of mind with a $50 travel credit when they book their first trip for a new Savoya traveler. They use your link to request a meeting with our sales team to get started.

Step 3

Earn

Once your referral reaches their third Savoya trip within the first six months, you will automatically receive your choice of reward up to a $500 value. The more you refer, the more you earn!
