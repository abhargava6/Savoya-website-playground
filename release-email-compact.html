Release Notes
June 9, 2026
Smarter calendar invites, new fraud controls, margin protection during special events — plus two fixes for the teams reviewing reservations and resolving cases.
ⓘ Going live Tuesday, June 9   3 enhancements · 2 fixes
Enhancement
Calendar invites that match the ride
Invites now block pickup to estimated final drop-off — not flight departure — and carry the full itinerary: every stop with local times, flight details, per-stop notes, plus the Driver Details & Live Tracking link and the Client Care number.
◆ Impact — passengers and other stakeholders receiving the Savoya invite get full trip details, live driver tracking, and a direct line to us — just by having the invite on their calendar.

Enhancement
Fraud prevention controls
Profiles can now be deactivated as “Suspected fraud,” which auto-cancels their upcoming reservations behind a confirmation modal (in-progress trips aren't touched for now). First-time retail bookers can no longer swap the card on file once a booking is paid.
◆ Impact — Ops responds to fraud in one action instead of manual cancel/rebook, and the stolen-card swap loophole driving active fraud losses is closed.

Enhancement
Special-event discount protection
Retail bookings that fall inside a special-event window — think World Cup — no longer receive the retail discount. Outside event windows nothing changes, and non-retail clients are unaffected.
◆ Impact — protects vendor supply and margin exactly when demand peaks and we can't afford to sell at a discount.

Bug Fix
Audit Trail no longer flooded with duplicates
Silent trip-modified push notifications were logging dozens to hundreds of identical audit entries per event. Those entries are now suppressed, so the trail shows what actually matters.
◆ Impact — Ops, CSM, Vendor, and Billing teams get a readable reservation history instead of scrolling past hundreds of duplicates.

Bug Fix
Case resolution notes reach the right vendor
Vendor-fault resolution notes were appearing on the portal of whichever vendor currently held the trip — including the vendor who stepped in to save it. Notes now show only to the at-fault vendor.
◆ Impact — reliable vendors who pick up last-minute trips no longer see fault notes meant for someone else — protecting relationships we depend on.
