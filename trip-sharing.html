Trip Sharing
Your clients can now give real-time trip visibility to the people who need it — other TCs, passengers, security teams, group contacts, and coordinators — securely, instantly, through the Savoya app.
Who can share
▸ Travel Coordinators
▸ Company Managers
▸ Team Members
Who can view
▸ Other TCs
▸ Passengers
▸ Security Teams
▸ Group Contacts
▸ Coordinators & others
📱 Available exclusively on the Savoya app — not yet on the Client Portal
ⓘ The update will be available tomorrow on the App Store and Google Play. Clients will need to update their app to access these features.
1
Clients share from any reservation
TCs open a reservation and choose how to share — static trip details or a live tracking link — from one streamlined entry point.
● One unified entry point
● Static details or live tracking
● Email lookup + new user registration
2
Stakeholders open the app to track
The secure link is delivered via SMS, email, or any messaging platform. If they already have the app, they're taken straight to the shared trip. New users are routed to the app store first.
● Dedicated Shared Trips tab on Home
● Deep-link to app or app store
● Passengers see it too — same tab, same access
3
Live tracking with full trip context
Stakeholders see the vehicle on a live map with driver details, ETA, pickup/drop-off, and trip status — all read-only, secured by OTP.
● Live GPS on a real map
● Driver photo, name & vehicle
● Pickup & drop-off with time details
4
One link for an entire group
TCs share all active trips in a group with a single link. New trips auto-included. Full viewer management with copy-link and revoke.
● One link replaces dozens of shares
● New group trips auto-included
● Copy link & revoke per viewer
5
Every share is a lead
When a TC shares with a new stakeholder, we capture their contact details — name, email, phone. A built-in pipeline for identifying potential clients.
💡 Want to convert a viewer lead into a client? Reach out to Product for next steps.
Built-in safeguards
◆ OTP-secured
◆ Auto-cleanup
◆ Short URLs
◆ Viewer mgmt
◆ SMS + email
◆ Read-only
 
Also Shipping
Driver Location Warnings
When a chauffeur's GPS is unavailable or outdated, the app now surfaces a clear warning — so clients and stakeholders know exactly what they're looking at.
📱 App only — not yet on Client Portal
⚠ Location unavailable
Driver isn't using the app. Persistent warning on all maps including Live Ops.
📍 Location outdated
Driver goes out of network or stops sharing GPS. Auto-clears when signal resumes.
How it works
● Warning on all maps including Live Ops
● Dismissible banner with clear messaging
● Stale threshold: 2 min without GPS update
● No warning on completed or billed trips
