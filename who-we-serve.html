Frequently Asked Questions

What is Savoya?
Savoya is a premium black car and executive ground transportation platform trusted by 61% of Fortune 100 companies. We connect discerning travelers with highly vetted, professional chauffeurs across 350+ markets worldwide.
Does Savoya require a contract or minimum commitment?
No long-term contracts are required to get started. You can begin booking individual trips or contact our team to set up a corporate account -- whichever fits your needs.
How does Savoya vet its chauffeurs?
Every chauffeur on the Savoya platform passes a rigorous 14-point vetting process, including comprehensive background checks, drug screening, and a signed confidentiality agreement. Less than 2% of applicants earn a place on our platform.
How is Savoya different from other executive and black car services?
Savoya operates in a category of its own. While other services function as marketplaces or aggregators, Savoya is a fully managed platform with proprietary technology, dedicated account management, and an obsessive focus on the client experience from booking to destination.
How much does Savoya cost?
Savoya's pricing reflects the premium standard of service -- vetted chauffeurs, dedicated account management, real-time technology, and 24/7 live Client Care. Contact our team for a personalized quote based on your travel needs.
Where does Savoya operate?
Savoya operates in 350+ markets worldwide, covering major cities and corporate travel hubs across North America, Europe, Asia, the Middle East, Africa, and beyond.
