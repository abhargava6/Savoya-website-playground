Dynamic Tech Solutions

Take Control
of Your Travel

With Savoya's proprietary platform, we provide you with the tools to manage and customize your travel from departure to arrival. Technology is at the core of our service, and we're committed to evolving to serve your needs because your peace of mind is our standard for success.

Stay Informed

Customizable Notifications

Up-to-the-minute trip status updates via text, email, or flight integration keep you informed at every stage of the journey.

  • Advanced driver and vehicle details shared ahead of time for added security

  • Customized text and email alerts tailored for each recipient

  • Control notification delivery at key milestones: On Location, On Board, Arrival, and Trip Complete

Streamlined Management

Online Portal

Manage your travel in a user-friendly, single, searchable dashboard designed for efficiency and control.

  • Custom traveler profiles with saved preferences for a personalized experience

  • Address auto-fills, saved locations, and single-click re-booking

  • Reporting tools for reconciling charges and tracking usage across your organization

Real-Time Visibility

GPS Tracking

Best-in-class real-time tracking gives you complete visibility into every trip, from pickup to destination.

  • View chauffeur location on COREview and the mobile app in real time

  • Dynamic ETA updates based on live traffic conditions

  • Multi-person access for seamless team coordination and oversight

On-The-Go Access

Mobile App

Modify existing itineraries, contact your driver, and monitor real-time progress directly from your iOS or Android device.

  • Available on both iOS and Android for maximum flexibility

  • Obscured communications protect private contact information

  • Book, manage, and track trips from anywhere with full portal functionality
