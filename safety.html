Technology Designed for Peace of Mind

With Savoya, you will never have a random chauffeur assigned to your trip. Our Certified Chauffeur Program and proprietary tech ensure you travel exclusively with vetted chauffeurs.

Advance Chauffeur and Vehicle Details

Savoya's industry-leading notifications provide you with your driver's name, phone, photo, and vehicle details within 24 hours before the start of your trip.

Chauffeur Screening and Certification

Every Savoya chauffeur completes a multi-stage vetting process before getting behind the wheel to ensure your assets remain secure.

Obscured Traveler Communications

Every call and message made through the Savoya mobile app hides travelers' personal contact information for increased privacy.

Our Trusted Chauffeur Partners

Discreet sophistication is the hallmark of the Savoya traveler experience, combining style and safety in every detail. That's why we ensure our clients only interface with chauffeurs who embody the core traits required to deliver an elite level of personalized, consultative service.

Chauffeur Vetting Process

Every Savoya chauffeur completes a 14-point vetting process, including a thorough background check, verifying insurance and regulation requirements, and signed confidentiality agreements.

Identify Excellent Chauffeurs

We only seek chauffeurs with extensive experience, impeccable safety records, and a strong commitment to customer service.

Formal Agreement

All partners agree to uphold quality standards, confidentiality agreements, and maintain insurance levels.

Questionnaire and Screening

We disqualify 85% of applicants from our process as they do not meet Savoya's standards.

Technology Adoption

We incentivize partners to utilize GPS, trip notifications, early arrivals, and other factors that impact service quality.

Compliance Verification

We conduct yearly background checks, drug screenings, insurance verifications, licensing, permits, and social media audits.

Ongoing Mystery Shopping

Savoya employees routinely mystery shop our partners to ensure they are constantly delivering a high level of service.
