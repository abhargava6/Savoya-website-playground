Challenges Before Go-Live

Data Mismatch

Trip Type Definition

GS asks users to choose Hourly vs. Transfer upfront. Savoya determines this from the itinerary automatically. Presenting the right rate to the user when their system's definitions don't match ours is the core pricing challenge.

Data Quality

Address & Airport Edge Cases

Airports or cities that don't exist in our system will cause failures. We need safeguards and fallback processes for mismatches in location data.

Process Gap

TC & Passenger Detection

Mapping the Travel Coordinator and passenger profile from Concur data to our system's contact model requires client-specific configuration and testing.

Process Gap

QA & Flight Validation

Our standard QA and flight validation workflows need to be adapted for automated bookings that bypass the current manual intake.

Coverage Gap

No Service Market

If a client books a reservation in a market we don't serve, the integration will fail. We need safeguards to gracefully decline and communicate why the booking can't be fulfilled.

Availability

Pending / Limited Availability

When a client books a trip that's due soon or in a tight window, we may not be able to confirm immediately. We need a way to notify clients that their booking is not yet confirmed and is pending availability.

These are solvable — but each requires input from some combination of clients, GroundSpan, and our internal teams.

Two Paths Forward

We can solve these challenges iteratively while shipping, or comprehensively before shipping. Both get us to the same destination — the question is speed vs. upfront completeness.

Path Comparison

Recommended

Path 1: Ship & Iterate

  • + Immediately replaces manual CSV and email process
  • + Real-time pricing with special events from day one
  • + Feedback loop with clients and GS starts immediately
  • + Unlocks onboarding pipeline clients sooner
  • + Backward flow development runs in parallel
  • + Real usage starts generating inputs for backward flow design
  • + Naturally engages clients during onboarding — we learn their actual workflows and pain points firsthand
  • – Initial edge cases need monitoring and manual fallbacks
  • – Requires agreed interim solutions per challenge
VS

Path 2: Solve Everything First

  • + More polished experience at launch
  • + Fewer edge cases in production
  • – Requires extensive multi-party coordination upfront
  • – Manual CSV and email process continues indefinitely
  • – Timeline uncertain — depends on GS and client availability
  • – Delays onboarding new clients in the pipeline
  • – No real-world feedback until everything is solved

Why We Recommend Ship & Iterate

Immediate Value

Eliminates the manual CSV rate handoff and email-based booking process today. The ops team stops logging into the GS portal to re-enter data.

Accurate Pricing

Clients get real-time rates from our pricing engine instead of stale CSV data. Special event rates, dynamic pricing — all live from day one.

Faster Learning

Real-world usage will surface the edge cases that matter most. Talking to clients and GS with production data makes those conversations concrete and productive.

Pipeline Acceleration

An automated integration — even with known limitations — is more compelling to prospects than a manual email process. Moves pipeline clients forward.
