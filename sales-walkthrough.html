Quoting Assistant
A sales & CSM preview — how quoting a client could feel. Your feedback shapes it. (all prices below are illustrative examples)
Pilot · for Sales & CSM
Turn any client request into a polished, accurate quote — by just chatting.

Paste the client's email (or drop in a manifest), answer a few quick questions, and get a client-ready quote with real Savoya pricing — in minutes, not hours.

What it does

You talk to an assistant in plain English. It reads the request, asks you only what it needs, looks up the actual pricing from Savoya's system (your client's negotiated rates, special-event pricing, transfer rates — whatever applies), and hands you back a quote you can send. If a request can be fulfilled more than one way, it shows options to compare. You can apply a discount, a fuel surcharge, or add tax right in the chat and it updates instantly.

What it solves today

✗Hours per quote. Big multi-day, multi-city requests take forever to price by hand.
✗Rate hunting. Looking up rack, special-event, and transfer rates across markets one by one.
✗Easy-to-miss money. A special-event rate or a missing transfer rate that should've applied.
✗Manual documents. Rebuilding the same quote layout in a doc every time.
✗Guesswork on big groups. Figuring out how many vehicles of which type to move 120 people.

How it works — 3 steps

1

Paste the request

The client's email, a few lines of text, or a manifest — however it came in.

2

Answer a few questions

Just what's needed: which client, how many hours, any VIPs, which city, etc.

3

Get a client-ready quote

Review it, apply discount / fuel surcharge / tax, then send as a branded PDF, deck, or spreadsheet.
