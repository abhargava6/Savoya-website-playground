About Savoya

Savoya is a premium black car and executive ground transportation platform trusted by 61% of Fortune 100 companies. It combines vetted chauffeurs with technology including GPS tracking, flight monitoring, and 24/7 support.
Book via the portal or app. Our system matches you with a vetted driver and provides real-time monitoring with automated flight tracking and notifications at each stage of your trip.
Savoya operates in 350+ markets worldwide, covering major cities and corporate travel hubs across North America, Europe, Asia, the Middle East, Africa and beyond.
Savoya operates as a fully managed platform, not a marketplace. All drivers are individually vetted through a 14-point screening with signed confidentiality agreements. Every client gets a dedicated account manager and access to our 24/7 live Client Care team.
Savoya drivers operate luxury vehicles -- executive sedans, SUVs, Sprinter vans, and larger group vehicles including mini and coach buses.

Safety & Vetting

Every chauffeur on the Savoya platform passes a rigorous 14-point vetting process including background checks, drug testing, social media audits, and live screenings. Less than 2% of applicants are accepted; only the top 4% globally qualify.
Certified Groundwork chauffeurs are available in select markets with close protection and medical trauma response backgrounds.
Yes. Our Groundwork service offers drivers with close protection backgrounds emphasizing conflict avoidance and situational awareness.

Booking & Pricing

Pricing reflects the premium standard of service -- vetted chauffeurs, dedicated account management, real-time technology, and 24/7 live Client Care. Rates vary by market and vehicle type.
Hourly rates include minimum hours, multiple stops, and wait time. Transfer rates are one-way from airports to city centers; unscheduled stops may incur additional fees.
Minimums and cancellation policies vary by market and vehicle type. Details are provided during booking and sent via your confirmation email.
Black car service is not on demand like ride-share companies. Drivers' schedules fill weeks in advance, so booking early increases your chances of getting your preferred driver.
No long-term contracts required. You can book individual trips or set up a corporate account without minimum commitments.
Vehicle unavailability typically indicates the market doesn't support that type or it's currently sold out. Our support team may be able to source vehicles from other cities for you.
